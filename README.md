# Postings of the Republic 任免之譜

An aggregate, interactive visualization of government personnel actions in Republican China,
1912–1945 — appointments (任) and removals (免) announced in the government gazettes, from
the Provisional Government through the Beiyang years, the Nanjing decade, and the wartime
state.

**Live page:** `https://chengchengnyu.github.io/<repo>/` *(edit after enabling Pages)*

## What's in the explorer

A 34-year pulse chart of appointments and removals with era shading and hover breakdowns; a
ranked view of provinces and special municipalities named in post titles; the composition of
posts being filled (military commissions dominate the gazette's traffic); fingerprint cards
for the three regimes — volume, removal share, and how officials left; and the shapes of
careers in aggregate, from the 63% of officials who surface exactly once to the record-holder
with 85 gazette appearances.

## Data & method, briefly

Source: the **中華民國政府官職資料庫** (R.O.C. Government Officials Database, NCCU Library)
and the authors' calculations. Figures cover the full deduplicated record for 1912–1945
(262,250 actions, 119,092 distinct officials). For this analysis, the authors applied
rule-based deduplication methods based on name, date of appointment, and posting. **Results
are subject to change as the data improvement is underway.** Post-type and exit-reason
categories are keyword-based approximations; gazette survival and coverage vary across eras,
so year-to-year levels partly reflect the record rather than only the state.

## Data protection

**This page is a visualization, not a data release.** It contains no names, no individual
records, no post strings, and no dates finer than the year — only aggregate counts. The
underlying database is part of ongoing joint research and is not published here. Please do
not redistribute figures without the caveats above; for data or collaboration inquiries,
contact the authors.

## Technical notes

A single self-contained HTML file — no external libraries, no network requests, no tracking.
Open it locally or serve it from any static host (e.g. GitHub Pages).
