The Pulse of Appointment

Personnel actions per year — appointments (任) in jade, removals (免) in bronze, stacked. Hover a year for the breakdown. The gazette machinery hums through the Beiyang years, crests in the Nanjing decade, and thins sharply after 1937, even as the share of removals climbs.

§ 02the provinces · 省域

Where the Posts Were

Actions whose post names a province (jade) or special municipality (bronze), using the project's harmonization of Republican-era units (直隸→河北, 奉天→遼寧, …). Covers the 21% of actions carrying a place name — central and military posts mostly do not. Second figure: distinct officials.

§ 03the posts · 職缺

What Was Being Filled

Post titles classified by keyword. Half the gazette's personnel traffic is military commissions — the 授-rank announcements of a state at arms — followed by the clerical mass of section staff, and the county magistrates who governed the countryside.

§ 04three regimes · 時代

Era Fingerprints

The same machinery under three regimes. Volume per year, the removal share, and how officials left — each era has its own signature: the Beiyang churn, Nanjing's bureaucratic consolidation, and the wartime state that removed at triple the rate it could appoint.

§ 05careers · 宦跡

Careers, in Aggregate

Following officials across all their gazette appearances, 1912–1945. Most surface once and vanish; a persistent minority builds long paper trails — one official appears 85 times. No individual careers are shown, only their shapes.

Postings of the Republic — 任免之譜 · aggregate visualization of Republican-era government personnel actions (1912–1945), from the R.O.C. Government Officials Database (中華民國政府官職資料庫, NCCU Library) and the authors' calculations.

Figures cover the full deduplicated record, 1912–1945: for this analysis, the authors applied rule-based deduplication methods based on name, date of appointment, and posting, and 16 era-mismatched rows were excluded as scrape noise. Results are subject to change as the data improvement is underway. Province extraction uses the project's harmonization map (leftmost match in the post string; ~21% of actions carry a place). Post-type and exit-reason classes are keyword-based approximations. Gazette survival and scrape completeness vary across eras — year-to-year levels partly reflect the record, not only the state.
What this page does not contain. No names, no individual records, no post strings, no dates finer than the year — only aggregate counts. The underlying database is part of ongoing joint research and is not published here. All figures are provisional and subject to change as the data improvement is underway.
